# Precedent privacy policy

Precedent is a Forge app for Jira and Confluence. It runs entirely on Atlassian's platform.

**What it reads.** Comments of Jira issues in the site where it is installed, the summary and description of new
issues, and pages of Confluence spaces that a project admin links. It does not read restricted comments or
restricted pages.

**What it stores.** Decisions found in that content (a short statement, the quote it came from and a link to the
source), their history, and the Atlassian account IDs of people who act on them. Everything is stored in Forge
storage, in the customer's Atlassian site data region.

**Processing by a language model.** Comment and page text is sent to a model through Atlassian's Forge LLM
service to recognise decisions and to compare new issues with current decisions. It is not sent to any other
service.

**Sharing.** The data is not sold or shared with anyone, and it never leaves Atlassian's platform.

**Retention and deletion.** A quote is hidden when its source is deleted, edited or restricted. Uninstalling the
app deletes its storage, following Atlassian's Forge data retention.
